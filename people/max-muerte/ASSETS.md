`assets/max-muerte-lettering.svg` is derived from the transparent lettering alpha in **Layer 2** of MJ's supplied `RED_Max_Muerte_FONT.psd` (3306 × 853, four layers). The glyph bounds are x104–3202, y82–770. Contours were traced at alpha >127 and simplified with a 1.5-source-pixel contour tolerance, with the original canvas coordinates retained. The SVG supplies a new red gradient and directional bevel lighting for this web composition. It does not claim pixel-identical reproduction of Photoshop's layer effects.
